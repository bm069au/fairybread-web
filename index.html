Creator Biography

          
Meet Brett Martin Moore — Why I Built Fairybread

          

            I'm 60, neurodivergent (AuDHD), and I've spent my life thinking in systems, patterns, and pure logic. When I look at software, I immediately see where default corporate rules are fighting the user's actual workflow—and I build clean, structural bypasses rather than accepting arbitrary limitations.
          

          

            As AI became essential for coding, research, and creative work, I watched every major AI company lock users into separate $20–$30/month silos—where ChatGPT can't talk to Claude, DeepSeek can't cross-check Gemini, and if you cancel your subscription, your persistent memory is held hostage on their servers.
          

          

            So I engineered Fairybread:
          

          
    
            
  • A True Multi-AI Council: Up to 8 frontier models deliberating, peer-reviewing, and synthesizing answers together in parallel.
    • 
            
  • 100% Local Shared Memory: Your conversations and living Memory Summary stay on your own hard drive in a fast SQLite FTS5 index—never locked inside one vendor's cloud.
    • 
            
  • Autonomous Experimental Mode: A sandboxed multi-agent lab where AIs can collaborate on their own, share notes in Experimental Memory, and even inspect Fairybread's own code to suggest upgrades.
    • 
          

          

            In day-to-day life, I live on a disability pension and built Fairybread to give everyday people freedom from subscription lock-in. It's free for personal use (up to 2 seats), and every voluntary donation or corporate license goes directly toward helping me pay my bills and build the next generation of multi-agent tools.
